Brand kit · Variant B

Paw mark

Small things wear the paw. Anything shown under 160 px carries only the paw-and-hands mark, cut pixel-for-pixel from the logo. From 160 px up, the full logo, unchanged.

Product-owner decision · not settled

This variant uses only part of the logo, the paw-and-hands mark, wherever the stamp is shown under 160 px: favicons, app icons, the watermark badge and the judging badge. Whether the mark may stand alone, without the F·O·A tiles and the name, is the product owner’s call. Nothing on this page means it has been agreed.

  • Also open: the word “FOA” in the badge is typed in Plus Jakarta Sans, not cut from the logo. Does a typed label next to the mark read as a second logo?
  • Also open: the seal (every certificate needs one, FR-STAMP-02; any seal is new artwork).
  • Ruled out here: a tri-colour ring around the app icon. It draws new shapes in the logo colours.

Mark tier · shown under 160 px

Favicons, app icons, the watermark and the judging badge. The paw-and-hands mark alone, on white.

Favicons: 16, 32, 48 px

Mark

The mark alone, on a white disc with transparent corners. On dark tab bars the disc gives the green a clean edge; on light bars it melts away and the green dot carries the icon.

Honest read at 16 px: the four toes merge into one white blob and the hands become a one-pixel smile. It is recognisable as “green dot with a white paw”, not as the drawing. At 32 px the toes separate.

App icons: 180 and maskable 512

Mark

Full-bleed white square with the mark centred: iOS shows transparent corners as black, and a maskable icon should fill its whole square. The phone draws the corner shape, not us.

Watermark and judging mark

Mark

Both are a white pill holding the mark plus a typed label. Public preview: “FOA”, bottom-right, 85%. Judging copy: “FOA JUDGING” and the Submission number, top-left, 55%, so a leaked judging image is never mistaken for a public one.

Same badge, three backgrounds

The pill makes the badge independent of the Artwork: the label stays above 11:1 on all three. On pale paper the pill’s own edge almost vanishes (1.05:1); the 16% hairline carries it. At 85% the mark’s green-on-white drops from 3.01:1 to about 2.60:1.

Honest notes

What breaks at 16 px
The green disc is 14 px; toes merge, the hands are a one-pixel line. It reads as a green dot with a white paw. The name “Friends of Animals” is gone from every small size: recognition rests on the tab title and the app label.
Contrast of the mark itself
Green on white inside the logo is 3.01:1, right at the 3:1 line. Logos are exempt from WCAG contrast, but at the badge’s 85% it falls to about 2.60:1. If the PO wants the mark crisper, set the badge to 100%.
Light and dark browsers
On a dark tab bar the white disc gives 16.10:1. On a light bar the disc disappears and the green meets the bar at 2.30:1: still a clear green dot, but no edge.
Soft at 512
The mark is a 134×129 px crop of a JPEG. The maskable icon enlarges it 1.985×, so edges are soft. Everything 180 px and under is a reduction and crisp. The real SVG from #6 fixes 512 with a one-line swap.
Nothing of the logo is cut
Round plates clip the square crop only where it is white O-ring: the darkest clipped pixel is 245/255 (badges: 245). No ink is removed, no colour changed.
Typed label
“FOA” and “FOA JUDGING” are set in Plus Jakarta Sans Bold, the site font. They are labels, not lifted letterforms; flagged above for the PO.
Weight
og-card.png is 864 KB; as JPEG q82 it is 108 KB. Recommend the worker writes OG as JPEG or WebP. This variant adds no client JavaScript (server components only). The framework baseline measured at setup (≈178 KB gzip) is already over the 170 KB route budget (NFR-01): reported, not fixed here.
Fonts in the worker
sharp draws text with Pango. On macOS its default backend ignored the bundled font and fell back to Helvetica without an error; build.mjs forces fontconfig. The Linux worker uses fontconfig anyway, but packages/brand must ship the two TTFs.

Regenerate: node apps/web/app/prototype/brand-kit/variants/b/build.mjs, then rm -rf apps/web/.next/cache/images. Logo pixels come from foa-logo-flat.png (stand-in until #6 lands).
