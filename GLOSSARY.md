# Friends of Animals (FOA) Platform

Student art competitions that build compassion for animals, with a public gallery, charity auctions and donations funding animal rescue.

## People

**Participant**:
A student who enters Competitions, often a minor, holding their own account with a Guardian's consent.
_Avoid_: Student (as a role name), artist, entrant, user

**Guardian**:
The parent or legal guardian who authorises a minor Participant's entry.
_Avoid_: Parent

**School Coordinator**:
The person at a School who manages its Participants' Submissions and shortlists them for FOA.
_Avoid_: School admin, teacher (Teacher is a separate, lesser School role)

## Competitions

**School**:
An educational institution that applies to FOA and, once verified, can enter its Participants' work.
_Avoid_: Institution, campus

**Competition**:
A themed, dated contest that Participants enter, individually or through their School.
_Avoid_: Contest, event, challenge

**Submission**:
One Participant's entry into one Competition: the Artwork plus its title, statement and consent flags.
_Avoid_: Entry, artwork (for the whole record)

**Artwork**:
The image (and, when required, the physical piece) inside a Submission.
_Avoid_: Using it to mean the Submission record
