# Friends of Animals (FOA) Platform

Student art competitions that build compassion for animals, with a public gallery, charity auctions and donations funding animal rescue.

## People

**Member**:
Anyone with an FOA account. Every sign-up starts as a Member and gains other roles from what they do.
_Avoid_: User (in UI copy), customer

**Participant**:
A student who enters Competitions, often a minor. May have their own account, be managed from a Guardian's account, or exist only on a School's Roster.
_Avoid_: Student (as a role name), artist, entrant, user

**Display Name**:
The name shown publicly for a Participant (gallery, results, auction credit); by default first name and last initial.
_Avoid_: Username, nickname

**Guardian**:
The parent or legal guardian who authorises a minor Participant's entry.
_Avoid_: Parent

**School Coordinator**:
The person at a School who manages its Participants' Submissions and shortlists them for FOA.
_Avoid_: School admin, teacher (Teacher is a separate, lesser School role)

## Competitions

**School**:
An educational institution that applies to FOA and, once verified, can enter its Participants' work.
_Avoid_: Institution, campus

**Competition**:
A themed, dated contest that Participants enter, individually or through their School.
_Avoid_: Contest, event, challenge

**Submission**:
One Participant's entry into one Competition: the Artwork plus its title, statement and consent flags.
_Avoid_: Entry, artwork (for the whole record)

**Artwork**:
The image (and, when required, the physical piece) inside a Submission.
_Avoid_: Using it to mean the Submission record

**Competition Registration**:
A School's or an individual Participant's sign-up for one Competition; carries the registration number.
_Avoid_: Enrolment (for the School-level record)

**Enrolment**:
One Participant's place in one Competition category, through their School's Competition Registration or their own.
_Avoid_: Entry, entrant, registration (for the per-Participant record)

**Roster**:
The list of a School's Enrolments for one Competition.
_Avoid_: Class list, student list

**Shortlist**:
The Enrolments a School selects, up to its limit per category, to send on to FOA judging.
_Avoid_: Selection, top entries

**Judging Round**:
One stage of FOA judging (city/district, state, national or global), scored digitally or after physical inspection.
_Avoid_: Level (on its own), stage

## Recognition

**Award**:
A recognition given to a Submission in a Judging Round: a rank, grade, medal or special mention.
_Avoid_: Prize (a Prize is the physical item that may come with an Award), result

**Certificate**:
A numbered, QR-verifiable document issued to a Participant, Coordinator, Judge or Volunteer.
_Avoid_: Award (for the document)

## Fundraising

**Auction Listing**:
A consented, physically verified Artwork offered for bidding.
_Avoid_: Auction item, product ("lot number" is UI copy only)

**Donation**:
Money given to an FOA fund, one-time or monthly.
_Avoid_: Contribution (for individual giving), payment (for the record)

**Sponsor**:
An organisation (company, CSR partner, foundation, institutional donor) supporting FOA; shown publicly only with its consent.
_Avoid_: Donor (for organisations), partner

## Welfare network

**Welfare Partner**:
An NGO, rescue organisation, shelter, vet clinic, independent rescuer or foster caregiver listed in FOA's directory.
_Avoid_: NGO (as the model name), organisation

**Adoption Listing**:
An animal offered for adoption by a Welfare Partner. FOA facilitates adoption; it does not run shelters.
_Avoid_: Pet profile, animal (for the record)

**Volunteer**:
A Member approved to give time to FOA (technology, content, outreach, competition management, events and more).
_Avoid_: Helper, intern
